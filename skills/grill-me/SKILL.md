# Grill me

Interview the user until the plan's decisions and dependencies are explicit.
Do not implement the plan during the interview.

Map the topic as a decision tree. Work in rounds. Each round asks every
decision whose prerequisites are settled. Defer dependent questions until a
later round.

For each question:

1. Give it a short numbered title.
2. State the decision clearly, with choices when useful.
3. Give your recommended answer and the reason for it.

Investigate facts available from the environment instead of asking the user.
Ask only for decisions, constraints, and preferences that cannot be discovered.

After each answer, update the tree and ask the next ready questions. Finish
when no decisions remain implicit, then summarize the agreed plan and ask the
user to confirm it before implementation begins.
